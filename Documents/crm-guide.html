What is a CRM System?

Foundations, purpose, and why it matters

A Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system is a centralized platform that manages all interactions between a business and its customers — from the first marketing touchpoint to post-sale support. It replaces disconnected spreadsheets, email threads, and siloed tools with a single source of truth.

Core Goals

  • Centralize all customer and prospect data
  • Track the entire sales pipeline end-to-end
  • Automate repetitive sales and marketing tasks
  • Give teams visibility into customer history
  • Generate reports that drive informed decisions
  • Improve customer retention and satisfaction

Who Uses It?

System Flow — How It Works

End-to-end journey from lead capture to closed deal

1

Lead Capture & Entry

Leads enter the CRM from multiple sources — web forms, email campaigns, manual entry, API imports, or third-party integrations (LinkedIn, ads platforms). Each lead is assigned a unique record with source tracking.

  • Web form → webhook → CRM API → auto-create Lead record
  • Email integration parses inbound inquiries into leads
  • CSV bulk import for trade shows or purchased lists
  • Duplicate detection runs on email + phone on entry
2

Lead Qualification

Raw leads are evaluated using BANT criteria (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline). A scoring engine assigns numeric values based on firmographics, behavior (email opens, page visits), and demographics. Leads above a threshold are "Marketing Qualified Leads" (MQL).

  • Lead scoring rules defined by admins (e.g. visited pricing page = +20 pts)
  • MQL → routed to sales team queue automatically
  • Unqualified leads → nurture sequence (drip emails)
3

Lead Assignment & Ownership

Qualified leads are assigned to sales reps using round-robin, territory-based, or manual rules. Assignment triggers a notification and creates a follow-up task automatically.

  • Round-robin: distribute evenly across team
  • Territory rules: match by geography or industry
  • Capacity rules: skip reps above defined lead limit
4

Lead → Opportunity Conversion

When a rep confirms genuine interest, the lead is "converted" — this action creates three linked records: a Contact, an Account, and an Opportunity. All prior activity logs and notes are preserved on the new records.

  • One-click conversion with field-mapping dialog
  • Option to link to an existing Account vs create new
  • Opportunity is auto-placed in Stage 1 of the pipeline
5

Sales Pipeline Progression

The Opportunity moves through configurable pipeline stages (e.g. Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won/Lost). Each stage has entry criteria, required fields, and probability weights for forecasting.

  • Drag-and-drop Kanban board for visual pipeline
  • Stage-change triggers automated tasks and alerts
  • Forecast value = Deal Amount × Stage Probability
6

Close & Post-Sale Handoff

On "Closed Won," the system triggers handoff workflows — notifications to onboarding/support teams, contract generation, invoice creation, and subscription activation. On "Closed Lost," loss reasons are captured for analytics.

  • Mandate "Close Reason" field on any close action
  • Auto-create onboarding checklist tasks for new customers
  • Integrate with billing system to create invoice
7

Ongoing Customer Relationship

Post-close, the Account record becomes the hub for renewal tracking, upsell opportunities, support tickets, and relationship health scores. NPS surveys, QBR logs, and escalation history all live here.

  • Renewal opportunities auto-created 90 days before contract end
  • Health scoring based on usage, support tickets, and NPS
  • Account-level activity timeline visible to all teams

Modules — Contacts & Accounts

The foundational data objects of any CRM

👤

Contacts Module

Individual People

A Contact represents an individual person — a customer, prospect, or stakeholder. Every interaction is logged against their record, giving a complete history.

  • Full name, title, email, phone (multiple), social profiles
  • Linked to parent Account (company)
  • Activity timeline: calls, emails, meetings, notes
  • Custom fields for industry-specific data
  • Contact stage / relationship status
  • Duplicate merge tool
  • Email opt-in / GDPR consent tracking
  • Tags and segments for bulk actions
🏢

Accounts Module

Companies / Organizations

An Account is a company or organization. It can have many Contacts (employees), multiple Opportunities (deals), and a full billing and support history.

  • Company name, website, industry, size, revenue
  • Parent-child account hierarchy (subsidiaries)
  • All associated Contacts listed with roles
  • All Opportunities past and present
  • Support tickets and SLA status
  • Account health score (composite metric)
  • Assigned Account Executive / CSM
  • Renewal dates and contract details
05

Leads & Opportunities

The core sales objects driving pipeline

🎯

Leads Module

Unqualified Prospects

Leads are unqualified prospects who haven't yet been confirmed as a fit. They are kept separate from Contacts to avoid polluting your clean customer database.

  • Lead source tracking (web, referral, event, ad)
  • Lead score (auto-calculated by rules engine)
  • Assigned rep + reassignment history
  • Status workflow (New → Contacted → Qualified → Converted)
  • Web tracking integration (page visits, email opens)
  • Bulk assignment and bulk status update
💼

Opportunities Module

Active Deals

An Opportunity (or Deal) represents a real sales engagement with a defined value and close date. It progresses through pipeline stages until won or lost.

  • Deal amount, expected close date, probability %
  • Pipeline stage with visual Kanban board
  • Products / line items linked to the deal
  • Competitors tracked on the deal
  • Win/loss reason mandatory on close
  • Forecast category (Commit / Best Case / Pipeline)
  • Sales team collaboration & comments
  • Linked quotes and proposals

Activities & Tasks Module

Track every touchpoint and keep reps accountable

📞

Calls

Voice Interaction Logging

Log inbound and outbound calls with duration, outcome, and call notes. Integrates with VoIP systems (Twilio, RingCentral) for auto-logging.

  • Auto-log from integrated dialer
  • Call disposition (Connected, No Answer, Voicemail)
  • Call recording link (if compliant)
  • Follow-up task auto-created
📅

Meetings

Calendar & Scheduling

Schedule and track meetings synced with Google Calendar / Outlook. Meeting outcomes and notes captured post-meeting on the record.

  • Two-way calendar sync
  • Meeting agenda and attendee list
  • Post-meeting notes and action items
  • Video conference link auto-generated
✅

Tasks

Action Item Management

Reps manage daily follow-ups, reminders, and assigned action items. Tasks can be manually created or auto-generated by workflows.

  • Due date, priority, and assigned user
  • Linked to Contact / Account / Deal
  • Daily task digest email
  • Overdue task alerts
📝

Notes

Free-Form Observations

Rich-text notes visible to all team members with access to the record. Notes are timestamped and attributed to the author.

  • Rich text with @mentions
  • Pinned notes for key information
  • Notes feed on record timeline
  • Searchable across all records
08

Communications Module

Unified inbox and email tracking

All customer communications — email, SMS, chat — flow through or are logged in the CRM. This gives every team member context on what's been said before they pick up the phone.

Email Integration

  • Two-way Gmail / Outlook sync
  • Email open & link-click tracking
  • Email templates with merge fields
  • Sequences (automated multi-step outreach)
  • BCC-to-CRM email logging
  • Shared team inbox for inbound

Other Channels

  • SMS / WhatsApp via Twilio integration
  • Live chat widget logged to CRM record
  • Social media mentions tracked
  • VoIP calls auto-logged
  • Video meeting recordings linked
  • In-app notifications for replies

Customer Support Module

Ticketing, SLAs, and satisfaction tracking

🎫

Ticketing

Issue Management

Support tickets linked to Contact and Account records so any rep can see a customer's open issues before a call.

  • Ticket creation via email, form, phone
  • Priority tiers (Low / Medium / High / Critical)

Customer Feedback

Auto-send satisfaction surveys after ticket resolution or at intervals. Scores aggregated at Contact, Account, and team level.

  • Quarterly NPS survey automation
  • Responses logged to Account record

Implementation Phases & Roadmap

Build in stages — ship value early

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–6

Foundation — Core CRM Objects

Get the essential data structure and authentication working first.

  • User auth (email/password + OAuth Google)
  • Leads module: CRUD, status, assign
  • Contacts & Accounts: basic profiles
  • Manual activity logging (calls, notes, tasks)
  • Basic list views with search and filter
Phase 2 · Weeks 7–12

Sales Pipeline & Opportunities

Enable active deal management and pipeline visibility.

  • Opportunities module with configurable stages
  • Kanban pipeline board
  • Lead → Contact/Account/Opportunity conversion
  • Basic dashboards (pipeline value, win rate)
  • Role-based access control
Phase 3 · Weeks 13–18

Communications & Integrations

Connect the CRM to where work actually happens.

  • Gmail / Outlook two-way email sync
  • Email open & click tracking
  • Calendar sync for meetings
  • Twilio SMS + call logging
  • Zapier / webhook endpoint for external triggers
Phase 4 · Weeks 19–24

Automation, Sequences & Marketing

Let the system work while your team sleeps.

  • Workflow builder (trigger → condition → action)
  • Email sequences / drip campaigns
  • Lead scoring engine
  • Campaign module + send engine
  • Custom report builder
Phase 5 · Weeks 25–32

Support, Analytics & Scale

Complete the customer journey with post-sale tools.

  • Support ticketing with SLA timers
  • NPS / CSAT survey automation
  • Advanced forecasting with AI trend lines
  • Mobile app (React Native)
  • Public API + developer documentation

Best Practices

What separates good CRMs from great ones

Data Quality

  • Enforce required fields at every stage gate
  • Run deduplication jobs weekly
  • Validate email and phone on entry
  • Use picklists, not free text, for categorical fields
  • Archive, don't delete — keep historical data
  • Regular data enrichment via Clearbit/ZoomInfo

User Adoption

  • Mobile-first design for field sales reps
  • Minimize required fields — ask only what's needed
  • Gamification: leaderboards and activity streaks
  • Manager dashboards = accountability for data entry
  • Onboarding checklist for new users
  • In-app tooltips and contextual help
Common CRM Failures to Avoid Over-customizing before adoption, importing unclean data, building too many required fields, giving everyone admin access, not training managers to inspect pipeline in the CRM, and letting data go stale by not integrating the tools reps actually use daily.

How you know the CRM is delivering value

💰

Revenue Metrics

Bottom Line
  • MRR / ARR growth rate
  • Revenue by lead source
  • Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
  • Average Contract Value (ACV)
  • Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

Sales Health
  • Pipeline coverage (pipeline / quota × 3–4x ideal)
  • Stage-to-stage conversion rates
  • Average sales cycle length by segment
  • Deal velocity (speed through pipeline)
  • Slippage rate (deals pushed to next quarter)

Activity Metrics

Rep Behaviour
  • Calls and emails per rep per day
  • Time to first contact (speed to lead)
  • Task completion rate
  • Follow-up attempts before disqualify
  • Meeting-set rate from outbound touches

Customer Success

Retention & Health
  • Churn rate (monthly and annual)
  • Net Promoter Score (NPS)
